      Roadmap to the platinum

      Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: the order to earn them in, in six stages. Story first, then strangers and activities, then the collectible sweep, then combat and chaos, and money and mop-up last — with 100% completion and the platinum at the very end.

      Missable trophies

      Every trophy below can be missed if its moment passes. One honest warning: the official descriptions publish no information about mission replay, so until the game confirms that story missions can be replayed, treat every story trophy as missable and play the story carefully. Partner moments (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) and one-off story beats (In The Beginning…, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There) are flagged for the same reason — the description gives no second chance.
